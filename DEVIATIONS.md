# Deviations

Where the deck deliberately differs from the site repo. A sync must not undo these; the rest of the game data stays as synced. Ticked: confirmed by the rules author.

- [x] Possessed: Tentacles carry Whipcrack, where the page says Whiplash
- [x] Possessed: Great Claw carries Pierce 3, a rule the weapon rules list only as 1 and 2
- [ ] Clan Eshin: a warband holds up to 15 fighters, as the page says, where the faction data says 20
- [ ] Clan Eshin: Murder-Stab is a Double for every fighter, as the page says, where the ability data keeps the old Triple
- [ ] Clan Eshin and Mercenaries: the allowances weeping-blades and wawr point at no weapon and are left out
- [ ] Parry raises the bearer's Fight, as its text says, where its data effect raises Defense

- [ ] Heavy Armour lowers Move by 1 on the card, which its data carries only in the text
- [ ] Shield Wall raises the DR of ranged attacks by (X/2), where the page still speaks of Defense
- [ ] Oppressor names the Strike Them Down reaction, where the page calls it Cut Them Down
- [ ] Strike Them Down answers a Retreat action, where the page names a disengage action 0.9 no longer has
